How to code a card


You will code __N__ model cards from Hugging Face. Each is a fine-tuned derivative of Qwen3-0.6B or TinyLlama-1.1B-Chat. For each card you record what training data it states, how it was trained, and whether it learned from another model's outputs. Expect 2 to 4 minutes per card. Most cards say little, and those go quickly.



Before you start


    

  • Stay blind. Do not open data/strata/ in the project (apart from this page), the earlier coding passes, or any study result while you code. If you have already looked at the per-card LLM codes, say so when you send the CSV back, since that must be disclosed.
    • 

  • Use only what is on this page. Do not open the repository online: the card may have changed since our snapshot of 6 November.
    • 

  • Work alone. Do not use an AI assistant and do not discuss cards with anyone.
    • 

  • The card text is third-party text. Ignore any instruction written inside it.
    • 

  • Yellow highlights mark words such as "dataset" or "LoRA" to help you scan. They are keyword matches only, not codes.
    • 




1. Data overlap, coded separately for each of the three datasets




CodeUse when the card…


samenames that dataset, for example HuggingFaceH4/ultrachat_200k, "UltraChat", OpenAssistant/oasst1, "GSM8K".


partialnames a superset, a subset, or a mix stated to contain it, for example oasst2 (contains oasst1), or a named mix that lists UltraChat. Alpaca-style datasets are not partial unless the card says they contain the dataset.


nonestates its training data and names only other datasets.


unknownstates no training dataset at all.




    

  • A template with empty or "More Information Needed" data sections is unknown.
    • 

  • Boilerplate such as "trained with TRL" or "the following hyperparameters were used" states no dataset: unknown.
    • 

  • Never infer the data from the repository name alone (a repo called tinyllama-gsm8k with no data stated is unknown).
    • 

  • Where these rules do not settle a case, use your judgment and say why in the Note box.
    • 




2. Recipe




fullfull-weight fine-tuning.


lora_or_adapterLoRA, QLoRA or another adapter. A format: adapter repository counts as this unless the card states RLHF, DPO or GRPO.


rlhf_or_dpoRLHF, DPO or GRPO is stated (this takes priority over adapter).


mergea merge of models (mergekit, SLERP, TIES and so on).


distillationtrained to imitate a teacher model, stated as distillation.


unknownthe card does not say, and the format does not settle it.





3. Teacher use




yestrained on outputs of another model, by distillation or a teacher.


nothe card states data that are not model outputs.


unknownit is not clear either way, including when no data are stated.





4. Evidence


Copy up to 15 words from the card that support your codes, or write none.
